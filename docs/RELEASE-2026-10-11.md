## Limits retained

Public research demo. Weather/air model output is separate from observed measurements; recent citizen reports are incomplete coverage. Scheduled GTFS is not live vehicle movement. Civic geometry is a dated public reference with no published observation epoch or explicit dataset open license in the inspected service metadata. No paid provider, live congestion, measured-air network, tide feed or subsidence layer was introduced. Existing building archive/provenance is unchanged. Current provider success is not an uptime guarantee.

## Deployment ownership
