## Terrain traps to avoid

[Jakarta Satu DTM_PUPR](https://jakartasatu.jakarta.go.id/server/rest/services/DTM_PUPR/ImageServer?f=pjson) is anonymously visible and reports 0.5pixel spacing, but its EPSG3857 extent is only about **449×756m**. It cannot supply a citywide terrain model. Vertical units, acquisition epoch and datum are unspecified in the inspected metadata.

[Official elevation bands](https://jakartasatu.jakarta.go.id/server/rest/services/Peta_Ketinggian_DKI_Jakarta/MapServer/0) contain **17 polygons** and a KETINGGIAN attribute. They may communicate broad elevation classes after domain inspection; they cannot resolve street-scale flow. [156 benchmark locations](https://jakartasatu.jakarta.go.id/server/rest/services/DSDA/Lokasi_Stasiun_Banchmark/FeatureServer/0) have name/latitude/longitude fields but no elevation or velocity field.

[Copernicus GLO30](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/COPERNICUS_DEM_GLO30_2024_1) is a 30m **surface** model including vegetation and structures, not bare ground. Its source acquisition window is 2010–2020; its EGM2008 zero does not imply local mean sea level. Keep its error/water masks and mandatory notices if acquired. A bathtub fill over this raster would remain an illustration, not a verified flood forecast.

[NASA OPERA DISP-S1](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/go/opera/products/) is documented for North America, not Jakarta. Do not promise an Indonesian subsidence layer from this ready-made product. Jakarta-specific Sentinel-1 processing or research outputs remain separate candidates. The [Sinking City research repository](https://github.com/zarashabrina/Jakarta-Sinking-City) documents a local 2016–2024 InSAR/GNSS pipeline and impervious-surface analysis; source availability, data license and compute requirements must be checked before reuse. No such processing was run here.

## Rain, soil and observations

[50 manual rain-gauge locations](https://jakartasatu.jakarta.go.id/server/rest/services/DSDA/Manual_Rain_Gauge/FeatureServer/0) supplement the existing automatic network, including upstream stations. Their schema contains inventory/editor fields, not rainfall observations. Exclude editor identities from public exports.

[BMKG Data Online](https://dataonline.bmkg.go.id/) returned its public landing page200; this audit did not acquire station series or assume an anonymous observation API. [IMERG](https://gpm.nasa.gov/data/directory) offers satellite precipitation estimates at 0.1degree and half-hour intervals. GIS accumulation and rate products use different units/scaling; PPS downloads require registration. The existing GIBS display is not a downloaded numeric rainfall grid. [Open-Meteo soil moisture](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs) uses model volumetric water content (m³/m³); the bounded Jakarta anonymous probe returned429. No soil-moisture values were admitted. Urban drainage/infiltration cannot be inferred from a coarse model cell alone.

## Population, utilities and movement

[BPS API](https://webapi.bps.go.id/developer/) returned200; the [official client](https://github.com/bps-statistics/stadata) requires an API token. Public [Jakarta BPS](https://jakarta.bps.go.id/id) tables remain a separate route. Prioritise a dated official denominator matched to the exact administrative boundaries; do not turn residents into an hourly crowd estimate. The existing 2023 population reference remains explicit.

The [UTILITAS folder](https://jakartasatu.jakarta.go.id/server/rest/services/UTILITAS?f=pjson) is public. Its inspected telecom layer reports15,964 features and editor/operational fields. Only metadata/counts were retained: no network geometry or editor identities were harvested. The [Data_AirBersih folder](https://jakartasatu.jakarta.go.id/server/rest/services/Data_AirBersih?f=pjson) returns HTTP200 with ArcGIS499 **Token Required**. This is restricted access, not empty infrastructure. Public transit schedules and camera locations already integrated are not live congestion, vehicle loading or passenger occupancy. A live crowding feed was not established in this audit.

Pantau Banjir timed out after18seconds in this bounded anonymous check. Existing BBWS/BPBD reference/live contracts were retained; this was not a complete rediscovery of every water authority endpoint. Existing civic complaint and public flood-report channels remain source-limited, not citywide surveys.

## Simulation readiness

A useful next scenario can couple official drain/polder geography, declared rain assumptions and catchment land-use classes. The conservation check must be visible: initial storage + rainfall/runoff inflow − outflow = final storage. Before presenting calibrated depth or arrival, obtain terrain with a reconciled vertical datum, drain/channel sections and invert heights, tidal boundary, pump/gate operation and capacities, and independent dated validation events. Missing inputs stay missing. Neither satellite water extent nor a moving marker supplies discharge.
