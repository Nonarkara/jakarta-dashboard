Reference features & map limits

Rail lines and stations are OpenStreetMap route relations for MRT Jakarta, LRT Jakarta, LRT Jabodebek and KRL Commuter Line, as mapped; they are not an operator network map. TransJakarta, street congestion, flood sensors and land subsidence are not in this atlas: no open live source is wired in.

Footprints: Overture Maps 2026-09-23 -- OpenStreetMap 2 237 918, Microsoft ML Buildings 432 048, Google Open Buildings 710 351. Heights in real metres, never scaled: 4 814 tagged heights (OSM / survey); 28 807 estimates from floor count × 3.3 m; 3 072 454 per building from Google Open Buildings 2.5D Temporal (2023, the 75th-percentile satellite height inside each footprint); 265 275 from GHSL built-up height (EU JRC, 2018, 100 m area average) where Google has none; 8 967 legacy type defaults with no satellite signal, now rendered as flat footprints with unknown height. Satellite heights are capped at max(6 m, four times the square root of footprint area). Counts describe the baked dataset; tile simplification omits some small features. The satellite model under-reads the tallest towers (it saturates near 100 m); in Jakarta most towers carry a height tagged in OpenStreetMap, which takes precedence. Autograph Tower and Monas are drawn from OSM outlines and parts at published heights.

Rail and waterways: OpenStreetMap as mapped. Basemap Esri World Imagery; air quality Open-Meteo / CAMS; rain Open-Meteo and RainViewer; satellite layers NASA GIBS and JMA Himawari; storms GDACS. Tiles streamed from R2 by Range request. Data keeps its source terms: Overture attribution, OpenStreetMap / ODbL, Google Open Buildings, GHSL, Open-Meteo / CAMS.
